Integrated-gradients attributions

Values are signed per-atom contributions to the logit difference from an all-padding baseline: red toward the positive class, green away from it. Panels within a molecule share a colour scale except the descriptor-pathway panel, which is scaled separately; each panel prints its own range. An attribution is a contribution to a sum, not a per-atom classification.
